Ghostly Max is here
- The AI features, already switched on. No API key to create, no provider to choose, no model to pick — cleanup, voice editing and meeting summaries work the moment you activate. $12 a month, or $108 a year.
- Your voice still never leaves your Mac. Transcription is on-device on every plan, exactly as before. Max sends text to a model, never audio. That is not a setting; it is how the app is built.
- Ask your transcripts. Question everything you have ever dictated, and every meeting you have recorded, in the My Notes screen. The search runs on your Mac and only the matching passages are sent — never your whole history.
- Ghostly learns your vocabulary. When you correct the same word twice, it works out which corrections are names, jargon and acronyms worth keeping, and applies them from then on. Only the word pairs are sent, never the sentences they came from.
- Encrypted sync across your Macs. Vocabulary, corrections, prompts, profiles and phrases, carried between machines and encrypted here before they leave. The passphrase is yours alone and we never receive it, so keep it somewhere safe — there is no reset.
- Manage billing from inside the app. Card, invoices and cancellation, in Settings then Account.

Free gets better too, and stays free
- You have dictated the length of what? 284 works, from Ozymandias at 111 words to the long end of the shelf, keyed to everything you have ever said. The sidebar shows the one you have passed and how far into the next you are, and the famous ones let you know when you cross them.
- Screenshot and dictate is clearer about what it does: it captures your screen alongside your question and hands both to whichever AI app you are in, ready to send.
- Faster, better meeting summaries, and better answers throughout.
- Still unlimited dictation, every engine, Meeting Mode, Open Mic, per-app profiles, custom vocabulary, on every Mac you own. Nothing that was free has moved behind the subscription, and nothing will.

If you already have an AI key
- Keep using it. Everything works exactly as it did, and Max is entirely optional.
- If you subscribe and ever reach the monthly fair-use limit, Ghostly quietly falls back to your own key instead of stopping. You can add one under Settings, Refinement, Personal API key.

Fixes
- Deactivating a device now removes its stored credential properly, instead of leaving it behind.
- Ghostly Max no longer appears as a provider option for installs that do not have it.
- Clearer messages when an AI request cannot be completed, in place of raw errors.
