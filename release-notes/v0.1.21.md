v0.1.21

Reliability

- Microphone auto-switches when your input device changes. Unplug an external mic mid-session and Ghostly now follows whatever input macOS switches to (typically the built-in mic) — no more reopening Settings to fix it. Plug the device back in and Ghostly picks it up again. The Microphone dropdown labels the follow-system entry "System Default".

- Apple Intelligence can't strand the recording overlay anymore. The on-device model occasionally hung under memory pressure, leaving the recording pill frozen on "Processing…" until force-quit. There's now a hard 60-second cap, and the pill recovers and pastes your raw transcription instead of silently dropping it.

Refined

- Cleaner AI output. Post-processing strips a wider range of label prefixes ("Transcript:", "Output:", "**Cleaned text:**", and similar) plus the input fences when small models echo them back. Built-in prompts (Improve Transcriptions, Developer, Email, Casual, Structured Notes) were retuned so on-device models stop emitting those preambles in the first place.

- Catches off-topic short answers. Apple Intelligence sometimes answered a short dictation as a question ("save the file" → "Press Cmd+S to save."). Ghostly now notices when the AI output diverges from a short input and pastes the raw transcription instead.
