v0.1.22

New

- AI refinement has a master on/off switch. Settings → AI Refinement now lets you disable cleanup entirely without losing your provider, model, or API key. Useful when you want pure on-device transcription and don't want to rewire config to get it. The switch is honored everywhere, including shortcuts that normally force refinement.

- First-run onboarding now asks how you want refinement: Apple Intelligence (recommended on supported Macs), bring-your-own API key, or no refinement. You can change it anytime in Settings.

Faster

- The first recording starts sooner. The transcription model and Silero voice-activity detector now warm up in the background at app launch, so the first push-to-talk doesn't pay the cold-load cost.

- The default trailing-audio buffer is now 100ms instead of 0. Voice activity detection occasionally clipped the last syllable when you released the shortcut; the small buffer captures it without adding perceptible paste latency.

Reliability

- Apple Intelligence hangs surface faster. The hard timeout drops from 90 seconds to 30. Typical on-device responses finish well under 15, so a 30-second ceiling means a stuck session recovers and pastes your raw transcription quickly instead of stranding the recording overlay on "Processing…".

Refined

- Onboarding, update, and AI Refinement settings got an accessibility and visual pass: keyboard focus rings, accessible labels on radio-style option lists, smoother download progress that's visible even at 0%, narrow-window safe modal layouts, and Lucide icons in place of emoji on the provider status card.
