Meetings name themselves
- A few minutes into a call, "Zoom meeting - 11/13/26" gives way to a name drawn from what is actually being discussed, like "Q3 Pricing Review with Acme".
- The name is revised once the meeting settles into its subject, and settled from the wrap-up when the call ends. The first few minutes are usually small talk, and a title shouldn't be about the weather.
- A name you type is never replaced. Clear it and Ghostly takes over naming again.
- A small sparkle marks a name Ghostly wrote. In the library, "Name with AI" names any past meeting on request.

Every meeting shows where it happened
- The real logo of the service sits beside each meeting: Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack, Discord, Webex, FaceTime, WhatsApp and more. It's in the panel, the call-detected prompt, the minimised pill and the library.
- Calls in a browser are filed under the service, not the browser. A Meet call in Chrome is Google Meet, and "By app" in the library groups it that way.

Ask the meeting anything, while it's happening
- The panel has a new Ask tab beside Notes. The live transcript stays in view above it, so you never have to choose between following the meeting and asking about it. ⌘K jumps straight there.
- One tap for the things people want mid-call: Catch me up, Action items, What should I ask?, Talking points, Read the room and Summarize. Once the call ends: a follow-up email ready to send, decisions, and the rest.
- Read the room says how the other side seems to feel (interest, concerns, objections, buying signals) and shows the quotes behind it.
- Answers stream in and point to the moment they rely on. Click a time and the transcript scrolls to that line and lights it.
- Select any line of the transcript to ask about just that. When someone says your name, the alert offers "What are they asking?".
- Copy an answer, or add it to your notes in one click. Each meeting keeps its own conversation.
- The catch-up shortcut now answers here.

A calmer, more capable meeting panel
- The clock, pause and End sit in the title bar now. When the call ends, Copy, Export and New meeting take their place. The row of buttons along the bottom is gone, and the transcript has the room.
- A header shows the meeting's name, where it's being held and when it started.
- The wrap-up arrives as a card you can copy or add to your notes.
- Scroll up to read back and a Live button brings you to the newest line. The call-detected prompt shows its countdown as a bar.

Open Mic has been removed
- The hands-free mode never worked reliably enough to recommend, so it's gone. Its settings and shortcut are cleared from your preferences automatically.
- Push-to-talk, the dictation shortcut and Warm Mic Mode are unchanged.
