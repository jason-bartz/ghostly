Dictate the moment you install
- A transcription model now ships inside Ghostly, so your first dictation works immediately instead of waiting on a download. A more accurate model downloads in the background and switches over on its own.

A new look, and light mode
- Ghostly has been rebuilt around a new design system: liquid-glass surfaces, a refined violet accent, and shimmer on the moments that matter.
- Light mode is here. Choose Dark, Light, or Follow System under Settings, Appearance. Dark remains the default.

Notes, reimagined
- New Timeline view shows what you dictated, when you said it, and which app you were in, on a single rail.
- New By app view groups your notes by where they were captured.
- Tags are now flat and colour-coded by app category.

Three models instead of seventeen
- The model picker now offers Fast, Balanced, and Most accurate. Every engine is still available under All models.

Health and support
- New Health screen checks microphone and accessibility permissions, your model files, your input device, and your AI provider, with a one-click fix for each.
- New diagnostics bundle saves your logs and settings to the Desktop for support. It never contains transcripts, audio, or API keys.
- Optional error reporting, off by default. Reports contain only the kind of failure and your app version, never what you said.

Also in this release
- Faster, more reliable model switching and a deterministic default model.
- The Achievements screen has been removed.
- Settings have been reorganised into three main screens with everything else behind Settings.
