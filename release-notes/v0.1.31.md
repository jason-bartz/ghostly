The meeting panel got quieter
- The transcript no longer stamps a name and a time onto every single line. A stretch where one person is talking is now their name once, with everything they said underneath it, and the time appears on whichever turn you point at.
- The buttons lost their outlines. One filled button for the thing you are most likely to press, and everything else stays quiet until you move over it.
- The panel's corners are round again. They were being drawn square by the blur sitting behind them, which at this opacity you could not see anyway.
