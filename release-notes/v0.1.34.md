The meeting panel has real window buttons
- Close, minimise and zoom now sit at the top left, where the buttons on every other Mac window are. They behave like the system's: grey while the panel is in the background, coloured and captioned the moment you point at them.
- Green fills the screen, and a second click puts the panel back exactly where it was. It stays a window rather than taking over a Space, so the call you are on is still behind it.
- The old × on the right is gone. Red does that job now.

Minimising leaves something behind
- The panel shrinks to a small pill instead of vanishing: a live waveform, the running time, and a dot that says you are still recording. A meeting that is still being captured should never be invisible.
- The waveform is the actual conversation, not just your microphone, so a glance is enough to know it is still working.
- Drag the pill anywhere you like. Click it to get the panel back as you left it — same size, same place in the transcript, same half-typed note.

Notes handle lists the way a notepad should
- Typing "* " or "- " starts a bullet, and Return carries it to the next line.
- "1. " starts a numbered list that counts on by itself, and adding a line in the middle renumbers the rest.
- Two Returns end the list, the same as anywhere else. Backspace just after a marker takes the marker off instead of the space.
- Tab and Shift-Tab move an item in and out a level, renumbering as they go.
- All of it works the same in the panel's notepad and in the meetings library, which were quietly different before.
