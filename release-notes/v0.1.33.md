Meeting Mode is now part of Ghostly Max
- Live call transcription, the floating panel, the meetings library and the wrap-up summaries have moved to the Max tier.
- The Meetings tab is still in the sidebar, marked Max. Selecting it opens the Max page — what the subscription includes and what it costs — rather than a locked pane that only tells you no.
- Meetings you have already recorded stay on your Mac and are not deleted. Opening them again needs Max.
- The tray entry and the meeting shortcut are hidden without a Max licence, and Ghostly no longer watches for calls in order to offer to capture them.
- Everything else on the free tier is unchanged and still uncapped: unlimited dictation, all 16 transcription models, Open Mic, per-app profiles, custom vocabulary, screenshot capture, and AI cleanup on Apple Intelligence with no key and no network.

The Ghostly Max page says what you get
- It opens with what the subscription adds rather than with what the free app is missing. "No API key to find, paste or pay for" described the absence of a chore; the page now leads with the models that do the work.
- Everything Max includes sits under one heading — "Everything in the free app, plus" — so the list reads as additions rather than as the definition of the tier.
- The AI behind Max is named rather than implied: the latest Anthropic models, picked per job and updated server-side, with nothing to install, connect or configure.
- Meeting Mode is listed there now, alongside Ask your transcripts, vocabulary learning and encrypted sync.
