Ask your transcripts has its own tab
- Ask has moved out of My Notes and into its own place in the sidebar, beside Notes and Meetings.
- It reads your notes and your meetings together. A switch at the top narrows it to one or the other when you already know which you mean.
- Answers arrive as formatted text rather than raw Markdown, so headings and lists look like headings and lists.
- Citations are clickable. Press a numbered reference inside the answer, or a source listed underneath it, and Ghostly opens that note or meeting and highlights it.
- Copy an answer, or export it as a text file with its sources listed at the bottom.
- The search still runs on this Mac. Only the passages that match your question are ever sent.
- Ask needs a cloud model, so where it used to show an error it now tells you it is a Max feature and shows you what that includes. Your own API key still works.

Ghostly Max, if you have not seen it yet
- Max is the AI already switched on: no provider to pick, no API key to find, paste or pay for. $12 a month, or $108 a year.
- Your audio still never leaves your Mac. Transcription runs on-device on every plan, paid or not. Max sends text, never sound.
- It picks the right model for each job, a fast one for cleanup and a stronger one for summaries and questions, and keeps that current without a settings screen for you to maintain.
- It learns your vocabulary. Correct the same word twice and Ghostly starts fixing it for you.
- Encrypted sync and restore across your Macs, for vocabulary, corrections, prompts, profiles and phrases. Your passphrase never reaches us, so keep it safe. We cannot reset it.
- There is now a page inside the app that lays all of this out, reachable from anywhere Max comes up, so you can read what it does before deciding. Billing, invoices and cancellation live in Settings, Account.

Free stays free
