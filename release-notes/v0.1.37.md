Ghostly Max, smoother from checkout to cancel
- Subscribing now activates Ghostly by itself: finish checkout, and the browser hands you straight back to the app with Max switched on. No copying a key out of an email.
- Your subscription is tied to the Macs you activate it on. Deactivate a Mac in Account and it stops using your Max allowance straight away.
- Ghostly checks your subscription every few hours, so a renewal or a change shows up without restarting the app.
- If your subscription ends, AI Refinement hands the provider choice back to you, so you can carry on with your own API key. The Account pane says why Max stopped and keeps the billing button.
- Account shows how much of the month's AI allowance you've used, as one percentage.
- Clearer messages: cancelling no longer says your payment failed, and when the AI declines a request, Ghostly says so.
