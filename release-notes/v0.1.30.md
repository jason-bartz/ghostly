Take notes during the meeting, and let Ghostly finish them
- The meeting panel is now two panes: the live transcript on top, a notepad underneath. Drag the divider to give whichever one you actually use more room, or fold the notepad away entirely. Where you leave it is remembered.
- Type the way you type in a meeting — half sentences, a number you did not quite catch, a heading with nothing under it yet. Bullet lists continue themselves on Enter.
- When the meeting ends, Ghostly offers to finish the notes from the transcript. It keeps your headings, in your order, with your wording, and fills in what you missed underneath them — then adds what was decided, who agreed to do what, and anything left open, if your notes did not already cover it.
- Both versions are kept and both stay editable. Your own notes are never rewritten, so a result you do not like costs you nothing and you can run it again.
- Notes and enhanced notes appear in the Meetings tab, are searchable, and come along in every export.
- Notes typed before you press Start follow you into the meeting you start next.

Open Mic actually works now
- Open Mic could capture your voice, split it into sentences, and then throw every one of them away. It never asked for the transcription model, so unless you armed it within five minutes of your last dictation the model had already been unloaded and each utterance failed against nothing.
- The model now loads when you open the mic, and stays loaded for as long as it is open. A quiet stretch mid-session no longer kills the rest of it.
- Meeting Mode had the same latent hole — it loaded the model at the start but a long silence could unload it — and that is closed too.
- When an utterance does fail, Ghostly says so instead of writing a line to a log file nobody reads. That silence is why this looked like a feature that simply did not exist.
- The submit phrase field no longer loses what you type. It saved on every keystroke, and those saves overwrote each other, so "send" could end up stored as something shorter or not at all.

Meeting summaries got their own voice
- Ending a meeting now writes a wrap-up rather than a catch-up. It used to reuse the "Where were we?" prompt, so the summary of a finished meeting opened with what was being discussed "now" and closed by suggesting a line to say into a call everyone had already left.
- The wrap-up is written as a record: what the meeting was about, the key points, what was decided, who agreed to do what, and anything left open.
- "Where were we?" scrolls its answer into view. It was rendering below a transcript you had usually scrolled away from, so the answer arrived off-screen and the button looked broken.

The meeting panel
- You can see when someone is mid-sentence. A line only reaches the transcript once its speaker pauses and the model has finished with it, so a long sentence left the panel looking stalled for several seconds. Three dots now sit under the transcript with the speaker's name on them, and say "Transcribing" while the queue clears.
- A real title bar, with the Ghostly mark and a live timer, and the whole strip drags.
- The meeting name sits in its own row with the pencil beside it, and paused / mic-only now read as small badges instead of full sentences eating a line of transcript each.
- When a meeting ends, the buttons become Start new meeting, Export, and Close — the three things you actually do with a finished transcript.
- Meetings can end themselves after a set stretch of silence, for the call you walked away from without pressing End. Off by default. There is an option to close the panel with it, though leaving it off means the wrap-up is waiting when you come back.
- The line count is gone.

Meetings use the AI you already chose
- If you have Ghostly Max, or you have set up your own provider, meeting summaries and live transcript cleanup now use it automatically. Picking your AI once was always the intent; picking it three times was not.
- The setting says which one it landed on, so "Automatic" is never a guess — including when that means meeting text is sent to your provider.
- You can still pin meetings to on-device, cloud, keywords-only or off. An explicit choice is left alone.

Smaller things
- Setup now covers Meeting Mode, so the feature is something you are shown rather than something you find.
- The permission cards during setup link straight to the right System Settings pane. macOS only ever asks once, so anyone who said no the first time — or who dismissed the dialog and sat watching a spinner — had no way forward from inside Ghostly.
- A space is now added after each transcription by default. Dictating two sentences in a row no longer welds them together.
- The recording pill should reappear for anyone it had gone missing for. It was created once at launch, and if no display was ready at that moment — waking on a dock, a clamshell setup, launching at login — it was never created at all, with no error and no retry for the rest of the session.
